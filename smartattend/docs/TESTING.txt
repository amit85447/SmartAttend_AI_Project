  CAUTION: these are celebrity news photos, not webcam selfies. Measure on your own staff and tune FACE_MATCH_THRESHOLD.

LIVENESS
  Head-turn logic tested on real photo pairs (correct direction passes, wrong direction fails) and a static photo shown twice is rejected.
  A simulated screen-replay (blur + moire) lowers the passive texture score.
  Passive texture check passed only 68% of tiny, upscaled LFW crops, so low-resolution images are scored harshly. Calibrate
  LIVENESS_THRESHOLD on your own webcam images. This is NOT certified anti-spoofing and was NOT tested against real printed photos or phone replays.

HOW TO READ THESE HONESTLY
  The demo data is fake and generated by simple rules, so these scores only prove the pipeline is correct and leakage-free
  (a test changes future rows and confirms past features do not change). Where a model does not beat the simple baseline, the
  report says so. Do not present synthetic-data scores as real-world performance.

CLEANING CHECK
  A messy copy of the data is built with known injected problems; the cleaner removes/flags exactly those counts (asserted in tests).

EXCEL
  The workbook is generated from database data. Formulas were recalculated in LibreOffice and the COUNTIFS/SUMIFS/AVERAGEIFS results
  were checked. XLOOKUP could not be evaluated there (older LibreOffice), so open the file in Excel 2021 / Microsoft 365 to confirm
  the name/department columns fill. Native PivotTables cannot be created by the library; Pivot_Guide sheet gives the steps.
